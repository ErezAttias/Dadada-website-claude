Good to know.

Which mattress fits?

Any US standard crib mattress, 52″ × 28″. Leave no more than two fingers' width (about one inch) between the mattress and the crib side.

Is the toddler bed kit included?

No. The toddler rail is sold separately for $120 and comes in matching finishes.

How long does assembly take?

About 30 minutes. Tools and printed instructions are in the box, and assembly videos are online.

When will it arrive?

It ships in one business day, free to the contiguous 48 states.

What does the warranty cover?

Two years against manufacturing defects, for the original purchaser from an authorized retailer.
