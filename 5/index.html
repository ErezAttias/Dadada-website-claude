Do I need a conversion kit?

For Soho, LaLa, Bliss and Jolly, the toddler rail is sold separately. Spirit comes with every conversion piece in the box, cradle mattress included.

Is a mattress included?

Our cribs fit any US standard crib mattress, sold separately. Spirit includes its cradle mattress.

How does shipping work?

Shipping is free to the contiguous 48 states. Everything ships to a street address, so no P.O. boxes, and each crib arrives with the tools you need to put it together.

Where are dadada cribs made?

In Italy, in family workshops, from kiln-dried European beech. Every crib is GREENGUARD Gold certified.
